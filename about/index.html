WHEN FEAR SPIKES

Steady me now

A button that is always visible, and a home-screen shortcut that skips straight to it. Ninety seconds: a breathing pacer, one anchoring verse, a short spoken prayer, a declaration. It ends by asking whether you are steadier, and chains another round if you are not.

It works with no signal, no voices installed, and storage blocked — it degrades to text and a breathing animation, never to a blank screen. Crisis-support resources stay visible throughout.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Features

Twenty journeys

The 30-day journey through the armour of Ephesians; focused tracks for fear of the unknown and fear at night; and seventeen four-day courage stories, each built on one biblical account — the furnace, the giant, the den, the storm, the shipwreck.

Where are you right now?

Say it plainly — "I have a court date", "I'm afraid for my child", "I don't know how I'm going to pay for it" — and the library opens the journey that meets it, at the first day you have not finished.

A prayer, composed

Choose a kind and an intention and Stand composes a whole prayer from a reviewed corpus, with room for a personal intention. Nothing is generated at the moment of use: every line it can ever say is written down and reviewable.

English and Spanish

Every prayer and every label on the prayer surface carries both languages, and your choice is remembered. The traditional prayers are grouped and named — those shared across the wider Christian tradition, and those that are specifically Roman Catholic devotions.

Guided audio

Every day and every prayer can be read aloud, with adjustable speed, a repeat mode, and a 5–30 minute sleep timer for night-time listening. Prayers pause where their own pacing says to, so you can pray along rather than listen.

Your own evidence

A one-tap check-in before and after — "where is your fear right now?" — builds a quiet ledger over time, so you can see your own average drop after prayer rather than take anyone's word for it.

A private journal

Notes on any day, gathered into one place you can read back and download as a text file. Favourites, completed days, and a streak that survives switching between journeys.

Yours to keep

Back everything up as a file and restore it on another device, or erase all of it in one action. No account, no sign-up, no analytics following you.

HOW IT WORKS

Privacy, plainly

Stand has no account and no server to sign in to. Your notes, your progress, your check-ins, and your prayers stay in your own browser's storage on your own device. There is nothing for us to read, because none of it is sent anywhere.

That is also why backup is a file you download and keep, rather than a cloud we hold for you. The devotional content is reviewable in the open: the prayer corpus is a data file, and prayers are composed from it rather than generated, so what the app can say is knowable in advance.

Stand supports spiritual practice. It is not treatment for an anxiety disorder, and it points to real help when fear feels unmanageable.
