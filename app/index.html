If fear feels bigger than you can carry right now, you don't have to carry it alone:

  • 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text, 24/7)
  • Text HOME to 741741 — Crisis Text Line
  • 911 — if you are in immediate physical danger

Stand offers spiritual encouragement. It isn't medical or crisis care, and reaching out for more help is its own act of courage.
