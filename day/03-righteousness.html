WEEK ONE · ESTABLISH THE GROUND

DAY 03

Righteousness

“Having put on the breastplate of righteousness.”

Ephesians 6:14

Reflection

Spiritual warfare is not only about identifying wrongdoing in others. It asks whether your own conduct matches the standard you expect from everyone else.

PRAY

Lord, search my motives. Expose hypocrisy, correct pride, remove vengeance, and strengthen integrity. Let my private character match my public words. May the right thing remain right even when nobody sees it.

Amen.

DECLARE

My character is measured by what I practice, not what I proclaim.
