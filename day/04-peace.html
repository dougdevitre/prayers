DAY 04

Peace

“Have your feet fitted with the readiness that comes from the gospel of peace.”

Ephesians 6:15

Reflection

Peace is not passivity. It can require boundaries, hard conversations, repair, or walking away. Peace refuses unnecessary chaos without hiding from necessary truth.

PRAY

Prince of Peace, make me an instrument of peace without making me afraid of necessary conflict. Help me know when to speak, when to listen, when to act, and when to walk away.

Amen.

DECLARE

I can carry peace into a place without surrendering truth.
