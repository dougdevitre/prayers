DAY 05

Faith

“Take up the shield of faith, with which you can extinguish all the flaming arrows.”

Ephesians 6:16

Reflection

Faith does not pretend difficulty is unreal. Faith refuses to give difficulty ultimate authority. It makes room for a next faithful step before the whole path is visible.

PRAY

God, there are outcomes I cannot control, questions I cannot answer, and people I cannot change. I place them before You. Give me enough faith to take the next faithful step.

Amen.

DECLARE

This difficulty is real, but it does not control my character.
