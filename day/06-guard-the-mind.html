DAY 06

Guard the Mind

“Take the helmet of salvation.”

Ephesians 6:17

Reflection

Rumination can turn one painful moment into hundreds of imagined ones. Guarding the mind means deciding which thoughts deserve continued attention.

PRAY

God, protect my mind. Interrupt obsessive fear, challenge catastrophic thinking, and expose false narratives. Return me to what is true, useful, honorable, and within my responsibility.

Amen.

DECLARE

My mind does not need to solve everything today.
