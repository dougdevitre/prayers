DAY 07

The Word

“The sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.”

Ephesians 6:17

Reflection

Words shape interpretation, and interpretation shapes action. Sacred words can return you to courage when noise pulls you toward panic.

PRAY

Lord, place words of truth within me before the world fills me with noise. Let Scripture challenge fear, pride, hatred, despair, and temptation.

Amen.

DECLARE

Truth will remain after today's noise disappears.
