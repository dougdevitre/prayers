DAY 08

Fear

“The Lord is my light and my salvation—whom shall I fear?”

Psalm 27:1

Reflection

Fear predicts futures that have not happened. Courage returns to the responsibility directly in front of you.

PRAY

Lord, bring me back to today. Give me courage for today's responsibility rather than anxiety about tomorrow's possibilities. I will prepare wisely, but I will not worship fear.

Amen.

DECLARE

Fear may speak, but it does not get the final word.
