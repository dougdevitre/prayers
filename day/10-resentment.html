DAY 10

Resentment

“Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.”

Romans 12:21

Reflection

Resentment keeps another person's wrongdoing active inside you. Releasing it does not excuse harm; it refuses to let harm reproduce itself.

PRAY

Lord, I refuse to carry another person's wrongdoing inside me indefinitely. Help me pursue accountability without revenge. Let evil stop with me.

Amen.

DECLARE

What harmed me will not determine what I become.
