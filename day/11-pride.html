DAY 11

Pride

“Pride goes before destruction, a haughty spirit before a fall.”

Proverbs 16:18

Reflection

Pride turns uncertainty into performance and correction into threat. Humility creates freedom to learn, repair, and receive help.

PRAY

God, protect me from needing to win every argument. Give me freedom to say: I was wrong. I do not know. I need help. Please forgive me.

Amen.

DECLARE

I do not lose dignity when I become teachable.
