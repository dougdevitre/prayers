WEEK TWO · CONFRONT THE INNER BATTLE

DAY 13

Despair

“Hope in God! For I shall still praise him.”

Psalm 42:11

Reflection

Feelings are powerful witnesses, but imperfect prophets. Despair speaks in absolutes; hope looks for the next constructive step.

PRAY

God, when my feelings tell me nothing will improve, remind me that this moment is not the whole story. Give me enough hope for one more faithful step.

Amen.

DECLARE

I do not need all the hope—only enough for the next step.
