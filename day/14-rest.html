DAY 14

Rest

“Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.”

Matthew 11:28

Reflection

Exhaustion is not proof of faithfulness. Rest restores perspective, restraint, attention, and the ability to love well.

PRAY

Lord, I surrender the belief that exhaustion proves devotion. Teach me to rest without guilt. Restore my body, quiet my mind, and renew my spirit.

Amen.

DECLARE

Rest is part of faithfulness, not a departure from it.
