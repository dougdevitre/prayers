WEEK THREE · COMBAT IN RELATIONSHIPS

DAY 17

Boundaries

“Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it.”

Proverbs 4:23

Reflection

Forgiveness does not require unlimited access. A healthy boundary protects peace and responsibility; punishment tries to control another person's pain.

PRAY

God, teach me to establish boundaries without hatred. Remove bitterness without removing discernment. Let forgiveness free my heart while wisdom guards the door.

Amen.

DECLARE

I can love someone without granting unsafe access.
