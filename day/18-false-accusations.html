WEEK THREE · COMBAT IN RELATIONSHIPS

DAY 18

False Accusations

“He will make your righteousness shine out like light, and your justice as the noon day sun.”

Psalm 37:6

Reflection

You can answer falsehood with calm facts without making every opinion your assignment. Document what matters; release what you cannot control.

PRAY

Lord, when misunderstood, keep me from destroying my peace trying to control every perception. Help me speak truth, correct what is false, accept what is mine, and release the rest.

Amen.

DECLARE

An accusation does not define my identity.
