WEEK THREE · COMBAT IN RELATIONSHIPS

DAY 19

Forgiveness

“Forgive as the Lord forgave you.”

Colossians 3:13

Reflection

Forgiveness can be a process. It releases the desire for revenge; it does not erase memory, accountability, consequences, or wise distance.

PRAY

God, begin the work of forgiveness within me. Remove my desire to see another suffer merely because I suffered. Keep wisdom and boundaries intact.

Amen.

DECLARE

I release vengeance while preserving truth and responsibility.

TODAY'S PRACTICE

Pray for freedom from resentment, not forced reconciliation.
