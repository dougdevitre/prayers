WEEK THREE · COMBAT IN RELATIONSHIPS

DAY 20

Love Your Enemy

“Love your enemies, bless those who curse you, do good to those who hate you, and pray for those who mistreat you.”

Matthew 5:44

Reflection

Loving an enemy does not mean calling harm good. It means refusing dehumanization while seeking protection, truth, and accountability.

PRAY

Lord, teach me to oppose harmful behavior without dehumanizing the person responsible. Protect my heart from becoming what hurt me.

Amen.

DECLARE

I can seek accountability without hatred.

→

TODAY'S PRACTICE

Refuse dehumanizing language about someone you oppose.
