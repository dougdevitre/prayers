WEEK THREE · COMBAT IN RELATIONSHIPS

DAY 21

Protect the Vulnerable

“Seek justice. Defend the oppressed.”

Isaiah 1:17

Reflection

Strength becomes holy when it protects rather than dominates. Advocacy serves another person's dignity instead of using their pain for recognition.

PRAY

God, make my strength useful to someone vulnerable. Give me courage to protect without controlling, advocate without exploiting, and serve without needing recognition.

Amen.

DECLARE

My strength is entrusted to me for service.
