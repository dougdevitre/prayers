DAY 22

Courage

“Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid.”

Joshua 1:9

Reflection

Courage is not the absence of fear. It is faithful movement while fear is present.

PRAY

God, give me enough courage to move while fear is present. Keep me from waiting for perfect confidence before doing what is right.

Amen.

DECLARE

I can be afraid and still be faithful.
