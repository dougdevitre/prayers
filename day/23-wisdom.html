DAY 23

Wisdom

“Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding.”

Proverbs 3:5–6

Reflection

Urgency can impersonate wisdom. Wise decisions create room for counsel, consequences, conscience, and time.

PRAY

Lord, slow me down enough to see clearly. Keep urgency from making choices that wisdom would refuse.

Amen.

DECLARE

I am permitted to pause before a non-emergency decision.
