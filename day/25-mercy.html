DAY 25

Mercy

“Be merciful, even as your Father is also merciful.”

Luke 6:36

Reflection

Mercy makes room for human weakness and repair without erasing responsibility. It is neither denial nor enabling.

PRAY

Lord, let mercy temper judgment without hiding truth. Teach me the difference between compassion and enabling.

Amen.

DECLARE

I can make room for repair while keeping healthy limits.
