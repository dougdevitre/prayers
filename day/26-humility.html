DAY 26

Humility

“In humility value others above yourselves.”

Philippians 2:3

Reflection

Humility is accurate self-knowledge: neither self-exaltation nor self-erasure. It listens for truth even when correction feels uncomfortable.

PRAY

God, make me teachable. When correction comes, help me examine it before rejecting it. Keep me secure enough to learn.

Amen.

DECLARE

Correction can refine me without defining me.
