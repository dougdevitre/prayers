DAY 28

Surrender

“Do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about itself.”

Matthew 6:34

Reflection

Surrender releases outcomes while retaining responsibility. It is not giving up; it is returning each burden to its proper owner.

PRAY

Father, I surrender outcomes while retaining responsibility. I will do what belongs to me and release what does not.

Amen.

DECLARE

I am responsible for faithfulness, not control of every outcome.
