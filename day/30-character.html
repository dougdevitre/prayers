DAY THIRTY · THE FINAL BATTLE IS CHARACTER

DAY 30

Character

“Pray in the Spirit on all occasions with all kinds of prayers and requests.”

Ephesians 6:18

Reflection

The deepest victory may not be defeating an enemy. It may be that hatred did not make you hateful, deception dishonest, fear cowardly, or suffering cruel.

PRAY

Father, clothe me in truth, guard my heart, direct my feet toward peace, strengthen my faith, protect my mind, and place Your Word within me. When I have done everything I know how to do, teach me to stand.

Amen.

DECLARE

Darkness around me will not become darkness within me.
