DAY 02

Where You Go

“Where you go, I will go; and where you stay, I will stay. Your people will be my people, and your God my God.”

Ruth 1:16

Reflection

Ruth committed to someone at the exact moment it made no practical sense — two widows, no income, no prospects. The most consequential choices are often made by people with nothing to bargain with.

PRAY

Lord, I have very little to offer anyone right now. Let me still choose someone, still show up, still commit — and let that be enough.

Amen.

DECLARE

Having nothing does not disqualify me from showing up.
