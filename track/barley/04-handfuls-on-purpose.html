COURAGE STORY · THE BARLEY FIELD · RUTH 1–2

DAY 04

Handfuls on Purpose

“Pull out some stalks for her from the bundles and leave them for her to pick up, and do not rebuke her.”

Ruth 2:16

Reflection

He arranged for extra grain to be dropped where she was working, and told his men not to shame her for gathering it. She still did the gathering — the help came in a form that left her dignity intact.

PRAY

God, make me the kind of person who helps that way: quietly, without spectacle, leaving people their dignity. And give me the grace to receive help in that form too.

Amen.

DECLARE

Help that protects dignity is the best kind to give and to take.
