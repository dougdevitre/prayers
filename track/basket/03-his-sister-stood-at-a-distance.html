DAY 03

His Sister Stood at a Distance

“His sister stood far off, to see what would be done to him.”

Exodus 2:4

Reflection

Someone stayed to watch. Not close enough to interfere, not far enough to abandon — which is the hardest posture a parent ever holds, and it is not the same as doing nothing.

PRAY

Lord, teach me to stay present without taking back control. Let my watching be love rather than anxiety, and give me the steadiness to remain at the right distance.

Amen.

DECLARE

Watching from a distance is not abandoning.
