COURAGE STORY · THE DEN · DANIEL 6

DAY 01

The Trap

“We will find no charge against this Daniel unless we find it against him concerning the law of his God.”

Daniel 6:5

Reflection

His accusers could not find wrongdoing, so they engineered a rule he would have to break. Being targeted is not proof you did wrong, and doing right is not a guarantee of being left alone.

PRAY

God, I am being measured by people looking for a reason. Keep me from living defensively, from shaping my days around their search. Let my conduct be clean and my mind be free.

Amen.

DECLARE

Being accused and being guilty are not the same thing.
