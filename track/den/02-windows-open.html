COURAGE STORY · THE DEN · DANIEL 6

DAY 02

Windows Open

“He kneeled on his knees three times a day, and prayed, and gave thanks before his God, as he did before.”

Daniel 6:10

Reflection

He did not escalate and he did not hide. He continued exactly as before. Under scrutiny, faithfulness often looks like the unremarkable repetition of what you were already doing.

PRAY

Lord, I am tempted either to perform my integrity or to conceal it. Let me simply keep going — the same prayers, the same standards, the same care — whether or not anyone is watching or waiting.

Amen.

DECLARE

I will keep doing the right thing at the same pace I always did.
