DAY 04

No Wound Found

“No wound was found on him, because he had trusted his God.”

Daniel 6:23

Reflection

The record of the night was not the roaring; it was the absence of a wound. Vindication came from what was true, not from how loudly he had defended himself.

PRAY

God, let the record speak. Free me from the need to win every conversation about my character, and let time and truth do the work I keep trying to do with argument.

Amen.

DECLARE

I do not have to prove with volume what time and truth will show.
