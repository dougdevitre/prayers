COURAGE STORY · THE FURNACE · DANIEL 3

DAY 02

Even If

“Our God is able to deliver us. But if not, let it be known to you that we will not serve your gods.”

Daniel 3:17–18

Reflection

Their courage did not depend on a guaranteed rescue. 'But if not' is the sentence that frees you: obedience decided before the outcome is known cannot be bought by fear later.

PRAY

Father, I want assurance before I will commit, and You are asking for commitment before assurance. Give me an 'even if' faith — one that holds when the answer is not the one I asked for.

Amen.

DECLARE

I will do what is right even if the outcome is not what I hoped.
