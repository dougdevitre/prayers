COURAGE STORY · THE FURNACE · DANIEL 3

DAY 04

No Smell of Smoke

“The fire had no power over their bodies, and the smell of fire was not on them.”

Daniel 3:27

Reflection

The detail the story lingers on is not the flames but the aftermath: no burns, no singeing, not even the smell. What you go through does not have to become what you carry.

PRAY

God, I do not want to come out of this carrying its smoke — bitter, brittle, suspicious of everyone. Do the deeper work: bring me out of this without it clinging to me.

Amen.

DECLARE

What I walk through does not have to become what I carry.
