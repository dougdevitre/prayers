COURAGE STORY · THE GIANT · 1 SAMUEL 17

DAY 01

Forty Days of Shouting

“The Philistine came near morning and evening, and presented himself forty days.”

1 Samuel 17:16

Reflection

An army stayed paralyzed for forty days because a threat kept repeating itself. Fear rarely needs new material; it just needs the same voice, morning and evening, until the whole camp organizes around it.

PRAY

Lord, something has been shouting at me every morning and every evening, and I have arranged my life around it. Quiet the repetition long enough for me to hear what is actually true.

Amen.

DECLARE

A threat repeated daily is still only one threat.
