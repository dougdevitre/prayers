COURAGE STORY · THE GIANT · 1 SAMUEL 17

DAY 02

Five Smooth Stones

“He chose for himself five smooth stones out of the brook.”

1 Samuel 17:40

Reflection

David refused armor that did not fit and took what he had already proven in ordinary work. Courage is rarely equipped with new weapons; it is equipped with tested ones.

PRAY

God, keep me from waiting for equipment I do not have. Show me what You have already trained me to do, and give me the humility to use small, proven things.

Amen.

DECLARE

I will use what I have actually practiced, not what looks impressive.
