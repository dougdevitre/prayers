COURAGE STORY · THE GIANT · 1 SAMUEL 17

DAY 03

Not by Sword or Spear

“The Lord does not save with sword and spear, for the battle is the Lord's.”

1 Samuel 17:47

Reflection

The outcome was not a measurement of size. Naming whose battle it is does not excuse you from the field; it relocates the weight from your shoulders to God's.

PRAY

Father, I have been carrying this as though everything depends on my strength. It is Your battle. Give me the part that is mine to do and take back the part that never was.

Amen.

DECLARE

The outcome belongs to God; the faithfulness belongs to me.
