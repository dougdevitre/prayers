COURAGE STORY · THE GIANT · 1 SAMUEL 17

DAY 04

He Ran Toward It

“David hurried and ran toward the army to meet the Philistine.”

1 Samuel 17:48

Reflection

The detail that changes everything is the direction. He ran toward the thing everyone else backed away from — not because fear was absent, but because delay had stopped helping.

PRAY

Lord, I have been slowly retreating from something that has to be faced. Give me the courage to close the distance today rather than rehearse it for another week.

Amen.

DECLARE

Facing it today costs less than avoiding it for another forty days.
