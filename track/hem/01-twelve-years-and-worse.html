COURAGE STORY · THE HEM · MARK 5

DAY 01

Twelve Years, and Worse

“She had suffered many things from many physicians, had spent all that she had, and was no better, but rather grew worse.”

Mark 5:26

Reflection

Twelve years, every specialist, all the money, and the condition still advancing. Chronic illness carries a second weight on top of the first: the exhaustion of having tried everything already.

PRAY

God, I am tired of explaining this, tired of trying again, and tired of hoping carefully. Meet me in the twelfth year, not just at the beginning when I still had energy for it.

Amen.

DECLARE

Being tired of hoping does not mean I have failed at faith.
