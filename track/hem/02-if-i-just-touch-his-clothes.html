COURAGE STORY · THE HEM · MARK 5

DAY 02

If I Just Touch His Clothes

“If I just touch his clothes, I will be made well.”

Mark 5:28

Reflection

She did not ask to be introduced. She came from behind, through a crowd, aiming for the edge of a garment — because when you have been dismissed enough times, you learn to reach in ways that will not be refused.

PRAY

Lord, I have stopped asking directly because of how often the asking has gone. Give me the nerve to reach once more, however small and sideways the reaching has to be.

Amen.

DECLARE

A small reach still counts as reaching.
