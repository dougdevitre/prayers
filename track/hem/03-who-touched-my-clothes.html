COURAGE STORY · THE HEM · MARK 5

DAY 03

Who Touched My Clothes?

“She came and fell down before him, and told him the whole truth.”

Mark 5:30–33

Reflection

She had wanted to take the healing and disappear. Instead she was asked to stand up in front of everyone and say the whole thing out loud — and that exposure, which she had dreaded for twelve years, is where the shame actually ended.

PRAY

Father, I have been hiding the part I am most ashamed of. Give me one safe place to tell the whole truth, and take the weight of the hiding off me.

Amen.

DECLARE

The part I am hiding is the part that most needs light.
