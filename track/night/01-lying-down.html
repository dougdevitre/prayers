NIGHT FEAR & SLEEP · FIVE DAYS

DAY 01

Lying Down

“In peace I will lie down and sleep, for you alone, Lord, make me dwell in safety.”

Psalm 4:8

Reflection

Sleep is an act of trust: for a few hours you stop managing the world, and it goes on being held. Lying down in peace is not denial — it is a handoff.

PRAY

Father, as I lie down I resign as night watchman. What I did not finish today, You are not worried about. Let my body rest under Your safety, not my vigilance.

Amen.

DECLARE

I can end this day; God does not end with it.
