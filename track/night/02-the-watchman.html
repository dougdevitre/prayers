NIGHT FEAR & SLEEP · FIVE DAYS

DAY 02

The Watchman

“He who keeps you will not slumber. Behold, he who keeps Israel will neither slumber nor sleep.”

Psalm 121:3–4

Reflection

Someone is up all night, and it does not have to be you. The watch is already staffed; your shift ends at bedtime.

PRAY

Lord, You stay awake so I do not have to. Take the night shift — the replaying, the pre-living, the listening for trouble — and let me sleep like someone who is guarded.

Amen.

DECLARE

The night watch is God's shift, not mine.

→

TODAY'S PRACTICE

When you wake in the night, say once: 'You are awake, so I don't have to be' — then let the thought pass.
