DAY 03

Thoughts at Midnight

“When anxiety was great within me, your consolation brought me joy.”

Psalm 94:19

Reflection

Midnight thoughts lie about their size; at 3 a.m. every problem doubles. Consolation does not solve the problem — it right-sizes it until morning can.

PRAY

God, my midnight mind exaggerates. Bring Your consolation into the loop of thoughts I cannot stop, and hold my questions until daylight, when they shrink to their true size.

Amen.

DECLARE

Nothing is decided at 3 a.m.

TODAY'S PRACTICE

Keep paper by the bed: write the worry down once, and let the page hold it until morning.
