DAY 04

Darkness Is Not Dark

“Even the darkness will not be dark to you; the night will shine like the day.”

Psalm 139:11–12

Reflection

Darkness changes what you can see, not what God can. The room at night is exactly as held as the room at noon.

PRAY

Father, the dark makes everything feel unsupervised. Remind my body of what my faith knows: night is only dark from my side. Stay near until I stop checking.

Amen.

DECLARE

The dark is not empty; it is watched.
