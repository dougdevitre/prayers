NIGHT FEAR & SLEEP · FIVE DAYS

DAY 05

New Mercies

“His compassion doesn’t fail. They are new every morning.”

Lamentations 3:22–23

Reflection

However the night went — restless, wakeful, afraid — morning does not carry it forward. Mercy is not a rollover balance; it is reissued at sunrise.

PRAY

God, thank You that hard nights do not tax new mornings. Whatever tonight brings, let me wake to mercies that keep no record of my worst hours.

Amen.

DECLARE

Morning mercy is new — last night does not bill it.
