Week one · Refuge

Day 07

Silence

“My soul, wait in silence for God alone, for my expectation is from him. He alone is my rock and my salvation, my fortress. I will not be shaken.”

Psalm 62:5–6

Reflection

David speaks to his own soul like a friend who needs settling. Silence here is not emptiness. It is the noise of rehearsing and bargaining set down, so you can wait for God alone rather than for every outcome at once.

Pray

God, my mind is crowded with rehearsed conversations and worst cases. For a few minutes I set them down. I wait for You alone. Let quiet become a place where I meet You, not a place I dread.

Amen.

Declare

In the quiet, I am not alone.

→

Today’s practice

Sit in silence for three minutes with a timer. When a worry speaks, answer it once with “for God alone,” and return to the quiet. Afterward, write one word for how it went.
