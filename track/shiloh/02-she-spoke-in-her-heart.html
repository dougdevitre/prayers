COURAGE STORY · SHILOH · 1 SAMUEL 1

DAY 02

She Spoke in Her Heart

“Hannah spoke in her heart. Only her lips moved, but her voice was not heard.”

1 Samuel 1:13

Reflection

She prayed so honestly that the priest assumed she was drunk. Real prayer is often unpresentable — and being misjudged while doing the most sincere thing you have done in years is its own wound.

PRAY

Lord, I would rather be misread by people while being honest with You than composed in front of both. Take this prayer in whatever shape it comes out.

Amen.

DECLARE

My prayer does not have to be presentable to be heard.
