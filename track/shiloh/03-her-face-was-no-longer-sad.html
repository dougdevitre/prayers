COURAGE STORY · SHILOH · 1 SAMUEL 1

DAY 03

Her Face Was No Longer Sad

“So the woman went her way and ate, and her face was no longer sad.”

1 Samuel 1:18

Reflection

Nothing had changed yet. The request was still outstanding, and she ate a meal and her expression lifted anyway. Peace arrived ahead of the answer, which is the only kind that can arrive during a long wait.

PRAY

Father, I have been postponing peace until the news is good. Give me some of it now, in the waiting, before anything is settled.

Amen.

DECLARE

Peace can arrive before the answer does.

TODAY'S PRACTICE

Do one ordinary good thing you have been deferring until this resolves — a meal, a walk, a plan with a friend.
