COURAGE STORY · THE SHIPWRECK · ACTS 27

DAY 01

Neither Sun Nor Stars

“When neither sun nor stars appeared for many days and the storm continued raging, all hope of being saved was at last abandoned.”

Acts 27:20

Reflection

Fourteen days with no sky means no navigation and no sense of time passing. The hardest part of a long crisis is rarely the first night; it is the featureless middle, when you can no longer tell whether you are moving.

PRAY

God, I have lost track of where I am in this. I cannot see anything to steer by. Hold the course I cannot see, and keep me from giving up in the middle.

Amen.

DECLARE

Losing my bearings does not mean I am lost.
