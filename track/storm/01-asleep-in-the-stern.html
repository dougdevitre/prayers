COURAGE STORY · THE STORM · MARK 4

DAY 01

Asleep in the Stern

“Teacher, do you not care that we are perishing?”

Mark 4:38

Reflection

The boat was filling and Jesus was asleep. Their fear was not only the water; it was the silence — the sense that no one was reacting with the urgency the moment deserved.

PRAY

Lord, I have prayed and nothing seems to be moving, and part of me reads that as indifference. I am telling You the truth about how this feels instead of pretending. Do not let my fear become the final word about Your character.

Amen.

DECLARE

Silence is not the same as absence.
