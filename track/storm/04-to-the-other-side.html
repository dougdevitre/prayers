COURAGE STORY · THE STORM · MARK 4

DAY 04

To the Other Side

“Let us go over to the other side.”

Mark 4:35

Reflection

The trip's destination was announced before the storm began. The storm changed the crossing; it never changed the instruction or the shore they were headed for.

PRAY

Lord, remind me what You said before all this started. Let the storm revise my timeline if it must, but not my direction. Get me to the other side.

Amen.

DECLARE

The storm can change my crossing without changing my destination.
