COURAGE STORY · THE THREE HUNDRED · JUDGES 6–7

DAY 03

Too Many

“The people with you are too many for me to give the Midianites into their hand.”

Judges 7:2

Reflection

He started with thirty-two thousand and was told it was too many. Fear counts resources and concludes it cannot be done; the reduction to three hundred says the size was never the point.

PRAY

Father, I keep asking for more before I will start — more money, more time, more certainty, more people. Show me what is actually sufficient and let me go with it.

Amen.

DECLARE

I have been waiting for more than this requires.
