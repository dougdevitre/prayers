COURAGE STORY · THE THREE HUNDRED · JUDGES 6–7

DAY 04

Torches Inside Jars

“They broke the pitchers and held the torches in their left hands.”

Judges 7:20

Reflection

The light had been burning the whole time, hidden inside clay. Nothing was added at the decisive moment — something was broken, and what had been carried quietly finally showed.

PRAY

God, I have spent a long time keeping the cracked parts covered. If what is inside is Yours, let it show through the break rather than in spite of it.

Amen.

DECLARE

What I carry shows most clearly where I am broken.
