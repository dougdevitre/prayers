DAY 01

Set Your House in Order

“Set your house in order, for you will die and not live.”

Isaiah 38:1

Reflection

A sentence from a doctor can reorganize a life in an afternoon. Hezekiah was told plainly, and the telling did not wait for him to be ready to hear it.

PRAY

God, I have been handed news I cannot unhear, and my mind will not stop running. Slow me down enough to face this hour rather than every hour after it.

Amen.

DECLARE

I only have to face today's part of this.
