COURAGE STORY · TURNED TO THE WALL · ISAIAH 38

DAY 02

He Turned His Face to the Wall

“Then Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and prayed, and wept bitterly.”

Isaiah 38:2–3

Reflection

He turned away from the room to pray, and he wept while doing it. There was no composure in it and no performance — which is what makes it a model rather than an embarrassment.

PRAY

Lord, I have been managing my face for everyone around me. Here, with You, let me stop. Take the fear and the tears without requiring me to arrange them first.

Amen.

DECLARE

I do not have to be composed in front of God.

→

TODAY'S PRACTICE

Take ten minutes alone and pray without managing how it sounds. Tears are part of the prayer, not an interruption of it.
