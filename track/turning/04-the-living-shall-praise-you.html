COURAGE STORY · TURNED TO THE WALL · ISAIAH 38

DAY 04

The Living Shall Praise You

“The living, the living, he shall praise you, as I do this day.”

Isaiah 38:19

Reflection

Afterward, he wrote it down. What he had learned in the worst week of his life became a text other frightened people could read, which is what suffering can become when it is not wasted.

PRAY

God, do not let this season pass through me without leaving something usable behind. Make what I am learning here into something that steadies someone after me.

Amen.

DECLARE

What I am learning here does not have to be wasted.
