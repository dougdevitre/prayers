FEAR OF THE UNKNOWN · FIVE DAYS

DAY 01

The Unwritten Page

“Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding.”

Proverbs 3:5–6

Reflection

Fear of the unknown writes confident endings to stories that have not begun. Trust does not demand the whole plot; it takes the next line with the Author.

PRAY

Father, I keep trying to read pages that have not been written. Teach me to trust You with the chapters I cannot see, and give me faithfulness for the page in front of me.

Amen.

DECLARE

I do not need to know the ending to trust the Author.
