FEAR OF THE UNKNOWN · FIVE DAYS

DAY 03

Light for the Next Step

“Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path.”

Psalm 119:105

Reflection

A lamp for feet lights steps, not horizons. Scripture rarely shows the whole road, but it never fails the next stretch of ground.

PRAY

God, when I strain to see the horizon, lower my eyes to the ground You have lit. Give me courage to take the step I can see instead of waiting for the map.

Amen.

DECLARE

Enough light for one step is enough light.
