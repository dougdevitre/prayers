FEAR OF THE UNKNOWN · FIVE DAYS

DAY 04

Held

“Don’t you be afraid, for I am with you. Don’t be dismayed, for I am your God.”

Isaiah 41:10

Reflection

The promise for the unknown is not a preview; it is a presence. Whatever tomorrow holds, you will not hold it alone.

PRAY

Father, You do not hand me the future — You hold my hand into it. When dread rises tonight or tomorrow, remind me that presence outweighs prediction.

Amen.

DECLARE

I am not walking into the unknown alone.
