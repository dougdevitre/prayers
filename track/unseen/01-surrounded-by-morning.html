COURAGE STORY · THE UNSEEN ARMY · 2 KINGS 6

DAY 01

Surrounded by Morning

“An army with horses and chariots surrounded the city. His servant said, Alas, my master! What shall we do?”

2 Kings 6:15

Reflection

He woke up, looked outside, and saw only the threat. That is an honest description of many mornings: the first thing the eyes find is what is arrayed against you.

PRAY

God, I wake up already counting what is against me. Before I add up the threats today, let me take an accurate count — of help, of provision, of what has already held.

Amen.

DECLARE

What I see first in the morning is not the whole inventory.
