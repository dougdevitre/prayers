COURAGE STORY · THE UNSEEN ARMY · 2 KINGS 6

DAY 02

More With Us

“Do not be afraid, for those who are with us are more than those who are with them.”

2 Kings 6:16

Reflection

Elisha did not argue that the army was small. He said the count was incomplete. Fear is often accurate about the threat and wrong about the total.

PRAY

Lord, my arithmetic keeps leaving You out and leaving out the people You have already given me. Correct the math. Let me feel outnumbered in the right direction.

Amen.

DECLARE

Fear counts the threat correctly and the help not at all.
