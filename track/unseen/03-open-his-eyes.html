COURAGE STORY · THE UNSEEN ARMY · 2 KINGS 6

DAY 03

Open His Eyes

“Lord, please open his eyes, that he may see.”

2 Kings 6:17

Reflection

The prayer was not for reinforcements but for sight. Nothing changed outside the city; what changed was what one frightened man could see of what was already there.

PRAY

Father, I am not asking You to send what You have already sent. Open my eyes. Show me the help that has been standing around me while I was busy counting chariots.

Amen.

DECLARE

The help I need may already be here, unseen.
