COURAGE STORY · THE WALL · NEHEMIAH 4 & 6

DAY 01

They Laughed at the Work

“If a fox climbed up what they are building, he would break down their stone wall.”

Nehemiah 4:3

Reflection

Before anyone attacked the wall, they mocked it. Contempt aimed at unfinished work is effective because early work always does look fragile — and the builder knows it better than the critics do.

PRAY

Lord, someone is making my effort sound ridiculous, and part of me agrees with them. Keep me building through the stage where the work cannot yet defend itself.

Amen.

DECLARE

Unfinished is not the same as worthless.
