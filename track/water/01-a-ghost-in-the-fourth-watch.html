COURAGE STORY · THE WATER · MATTHEW 14

DAY 01

A Ghost in the Fourth Watch

“They were troubled, saying, It is a ghost! And they cried out for fear.”

Matthew 14:26

Reflection

The help arrived and they screamed. Fear is not only afraid of bad things; when it has been afraid long enough, it misreads the rescue as one more threat.

PRAY

God, I have been frightened long enough to be suspicious of good news. Steady me enough to look twice at what is approaching before I decide what it is.

Amen.

DECLARE

Not everything that startles me is against me.
