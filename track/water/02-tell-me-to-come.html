COURAGE STORY · THE WATER · MATTHEW 14

DAY 02

Tell Me to Come

“Lord, if it is you, command me to come to you on the waters. And he said, Come.”

Matthew 14:28–29

Reflection

Peter asked for the harder thing. The safe option — stay in the boat with everyone else — was available the entire time and no one would have blamed him for taking it.

PRAY

Lord, I have been asking You to calm things down when part of me is being invited out. If this is You, say so, and give me the nerve to put a foot over the side.

Amen.

DECLARE

The safe option is not always the faithful one.
