COURAGE STORY · THE WATER · MATTHEW 14

DAY 04

Immediately He Reached Out

“Immediately Jesus stretched out his hand and took hold of him.”

Matthew 14:31

Reflection

No lecture came first. The hand came first, and the question came after, from inside the grip. Being caught is the part of the story fear never predicts.

PRAY

God, thank You that the reaching did not wait for me to recover my composure. When I sink again, remind me how quickly the hand came the last time.

Amen.

DECLARE

When I sank before, I was not left there.
