COURAGE STORY · THE LAST MEAL · 1 KINGS 17

DAY 01

A Handful of Meal

“I have only a handful of meal in a jar and a little oil in a jar. I am gathering two sticks, to go in and cook it for me and my son, that we may eat it and die.”

1 Kings 17:12

Reflection

She could see the bottom of the jar and she had done the arithmetic. Financial fear is rarely irrational — it is usually a correct calculation carried all the way to a conclusion no one has actually reached yet.

PRAY

God, I have counted what is left and I do not like the number. Keep me from living the ending before it arrives, and give me enough steadiness to get through today's part.

Amen.

DECLARE

I have counted correctly and still do not know the ending.

TODAY'S PRACTICE

Write the actual numbers down — what is there, what is owed, what is due when. Fear does worse math in the dark.
