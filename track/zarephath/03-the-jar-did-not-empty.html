COURAGE STORY · THE LAST MEAL · 1 KINGS 17

DAY 03

The Jar Did Not Empty

“The jar of meal was not emptied, and the jar of oil did not fail.”

1 Kings 17:16

Reflection

What she got was never a stockpile. It was a jar that kept not being empty — enough each time she went to it, and never more than that, which is a harder gift to trust than a lump sum.

PRAY

Father, I keep asking for a surplus so I can stop depending on You daily. Teach me to trust a jar that refills instead of a pile that reassures.

Amen.

DECLARE

Enough, repeatedly, is a real kind of provision.

Look back over the last twelve months and write down three times the jar did not empty — the unexpected work, the bill that shrank, the help that arrived.
