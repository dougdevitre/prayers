Good to know

Plain files, kept private

These are plain PDF files, with no tracking and nothing that runs when you open them. Stand offers spiritual encouragement and prayer. It is not medical, mental health, legal or financial advice, and it is not a substitute for a licensed professional. If you need help now, the numbers are in every file and in the app.
